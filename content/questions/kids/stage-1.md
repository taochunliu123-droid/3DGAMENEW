# 暴龍報告 | T. rex Report
答案：B

## 情境
小安要請 AI 幫他準備暴龍的上台報告。
## Scenario
An wants AI to help him prepare a talk about T. rex.

## 問題
哪一句才是 A「演誰」？
## Question
Which line is A (Act: who AI plays)?

## A
我是三年級，要上台講三分鐘。
## A (EN)
I'm in third grade and will talk for three minutes.

## B
你是很會講恐龍故事的博物館導覽員。
## B (EN)
You're a museum guide who tells great dinosaur stories.

## C
請用小朋友聽得懂的簡單句子。
## C (EN)
Use simple sentences kids can understand.

## D
請參考自然課本第 20 頁的介紹。
## D (EN)
Use page 20 of my science book.

## 解析
「你是…導覽員」是請 AI 扮演一個人，這就是「演誰」。「我是三年級」說的是小安自己，是背景。
## Why
"You're a museum guide" asks AI to play someone. "I'm in third grade" is about An himself — that's background.

## 提示
哪一句在說 AI，不是在說小安？
## Hint
Which line is about AI, not about An?

# 練英文 | English Practice
答案：D

## 情境
你想請 AI 陪你練英文。四句裡有三句是在請 AI 演一個角色。
## Scenario
You want to practice English with AI. Three lines ask AI to play a role.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 A「演誰」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT A (Act: who AI plays). Which one?

## A
你是在早餐店點餐的外國客人。
## A (EN)
You're a visitor ordering at a breakfast shop.

## B
請你當我的英文筆友，跟我聊天。
## B (EN)
Be my English pen pal and chat with me.

## C
你是很有耐心的英語家教老師。
## C (EN)
You're a patient English tutor.

## D
我的英文老師是從加拿大來的。
## D (EN)
My English teacher is from Canada.

## 解析
「我的老師是加拿大人」是在說你身邊的事，是背景。其他三句都是請 AI 變成某個人：客人、筆友、家教。
## Why
"My teacher is from Canada" is a fact about your life (background). The others turn AI into someone.

## 提示
哪一句沒有叫 AI 去當誰？
## Hint
Which line doesn't make AI be anyone?

# 猜動物 | Guess the Animal
答案：A

## 情境
你想跟 AI 玩猜動物，可是 AI 一開始就把答案說出來了。
## Scenario
You want to play "guess the animal", but AI tells you the answer right away.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
請你當猜謎遊戲的關主，換我來猜。
## A (EN)
Be the quiz host, and I'll do the guessing.

## B
我最喜歡的動物是企鵝和貓熊。
## B (EN)
My favorite animals are penguins and pandas.

## C
請用好玩、誇張的語氣跟我說話。
## C (EN)
Talk to me in a fun, silly way.

## D
這個遊戲要讓我玩得很開心。
## D (EN)
The game should be really fun for me.

## 解析
AI 不知道自己是出題的人，所以直接講答案。請它當關主，它就知道要出題、讓你猜。
## Why
AI didn't know it was the one asking. Make it the host and it will give clues and let you guess.

## 提示
遊戲裡，AI 應該是什麼身分？
## Hint
In the game, who should AI be?

# 第一次做蛋糕 | First Cake
答案：C

## 情境
你想跟媽媽一起做人生第一個蛋糕。
## Scenario
You want to bake your very first cake with Mom.

## 問題
請 AI 扮演哪個角色最適合？
## Question
Which role should AI play?

## A
你是五星級飯店裡最厲害的甜點主廚。
## A (EN)
You're the top pastry chef at a five-star hotel.

## B
你是很會吃、什麼甜點都吃過的美食家。
## B (EN)
You're a foodie who has tasted every dessert.

## C
你是很會教新手的烘焙老師。
## C (EN)
You're a baking teacher who's great with beginners.

## D
你是開了三十年麵包店的老闆。
## D (EN)
You're a bakery owner of thirty years.

## 解析
第一次做，最需要的是「會教新手」的人。主廚很厲害但做的是高級甜點，美食家只會吃，老闆忙著做生意。
## Why
Beginners need someone who teaches beginners. The chef makes fancy desserts, the foodie only tastes, the owner runs a shop.

## 提示
你需要會做的人，還是會「教」的人？
## Hint
Do you need someone who can bake, or who can teach?

# 讀書心得 | Book Chat
答案：A

## 情境
你想請 AI 陪你聊《小王子》。四句裡有三句在請 AI 演一個角色。
## Scenario
You want to chat with AI about The Little Prince. Three lines give AI a role.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 A「演誰」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT A (Act: who AI plays). Which one?

## A
請用像在說故事一樣的口氣回答。
## A (EN)
Answer as if you're telling a story.

## B
你是書裡的小王子，回答我的問題。
## B (EN)
You're the Little Prince; answer my questions.

## C
你是學校圖書館裡最愛看書的阿姨。
## C (EN)

# 班長演講 | Class Speech
答案：C

## 情境
你想練習班長選舉演講，AI 卻只會一直說「講得很好！」
## Scenario
You want to practice your class-leader speech, but AI only says "Great job!"

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
我的政見是每週換座位、多辦班級活動。
## A (EN)
My plan: switch seats weekly and hold more events.

## B
請用很有精神的語氣幫我改講稿。
## B (EN)
Fix my speech in an energetic tone.

## C
請你當台下最愛問問題的同學來考我。
## C (EN)
Be the classmate who asks the most questions, and test me.

## D
目標是讓我上台不緊張、不忘詞。
## D (EN)
The goal is no nerves and no forgotten lines.

## 解析
AI 一直當「啦啦隊」，所以只會稱讚。請它當愛問問題的同學，它就會挑戰你，讓你練到真的會。
## Why
AI is acting as a cheerleader. Make it a curious classmate and it will challenge you.

## 提示
誰會在台下考你？
## Hint
Who would quiz you from the audience?
