# 暴龍報告 | T. rex Report
答案：A

## 情境
你想請 AI 幫你認識暴龍。
## Scenario
You want AI to help you learn about the T. rex.

## 問題
哪一句最清楚說出要 AI 做什麼？
## Question
Which one clearly tells AI what to do?

## A
請用 5 句話介紹暴龍吃什麼、住在哪裡。
## A (EN)
In 5 sentences, tell me what a T. rex ate and where it lived.

## B
暴龍。
## B (EN)
T. rex.

## C
我好喜歡恐龍喔！
## C (EN)
I love dinosaurs so much!

## D
恐龍的英文怎麼唸？
## D (EN)
How do you say dinosaur in Chinese?

## 解析
有「介紹」這個動作，也說了要介紹什麼，AI 就不用猜。
## Why
It has an action (tell me) and says what to tell, so AI doesn't have to guess.

## 提示
找找看，哪一句有「要 AI 做的動作」？
## Hint
Look for the one with an action for AI to do.
