# 做餅乾 | Baking Cookies
答案：B

## 情境
你想和家人一起做餅乾。
## Scenario
You want to bake cookies with your family.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫一個餅乾的故事。
## A (EN)
Please write a story about cookies.

## B
請把做餅乾的方法分成步驟，一步寫一行。
## B (EN)
Please break the cookie recipe into steps, one per line.

## C
餅乾好吃嗎？
## C (EN)
Are cookies tasty?

## D
請推薦餅乾店。
## D (EN)
Recommend a cookie shop.

## 解析
分步驟，照著一步一步做就不會亂。
## Why
With steps, you can follow along one by one.

## 提示
怎樣最容易跟著做？
## Hint
What's easiest to follow?

# 檢查作業 | Checking Homework
答案：C

## 情境
你寫完作業，想知道有沒有錯。
## Scenario
You finished homework and want to know if it's right.

## 問題
哪一句最能學到東西？
## Question
Which one teaches you the most?

## A
請直接幫我改好。
## A (EN)
Just fix it for me.

## B
我寫得好嗎？
## B (EN)
Is my work good?

## C
請先檢查哪裡錯，再告訴我為什麼錯、怎麼改。
## C (EN)
First find mistakes, then tell me why they're wrong and how to fix them.

## D

## D (EN)
Do tomorrow's homework for me.

## 解析
先找錯、再說原因、最後改，你下次就不會再錯。
## Why
Find, explain, then fix — so you won't make the same mistake.

## 提示
你想學會，還是只要改好？
## Hint
Do you want to learn, or just have it fixed?
