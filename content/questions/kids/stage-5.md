# 取隊名 | Team Name
答案：A

## 情境
你們小隊要取一個隊名。
## Scenario
Your team needs a name.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我們想 5 個隊名，要像「閃電小隊」一樣短又有力。
## A (EN)
Please give us 5 team names, short and strong like 'Lightning Squad'.

## B
請想隊名。
## B (EN)
Please think of team names.

## C
請想很多很多隊名。
## C (EN)
Please think of lots and lots of names.

## D
隊名是什麼意思？
## D (EN)
What does team name mean?

## 解析
給一個例子，AI 就知道你喜歡什麼感覺的名字。
## Why
An example shows AI the feeling you like.

## 提示
可以給 AI 一個喜歡的例子嗎？
## Hint
Can you give AI an example you like?

# 只看課文 | Only the Text
答案：D

## 情境
老師說只能用課文回答問題。
## Scenario
Your teacher says answers must come only from the lesson text.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請上網找更多資料補充。
## A (EN)
Search the internet for more info.

## B
請盡量寫多一點。
## B (EN)
Write as much as possible.

## C
不知道的就自己猜。
## C (EN)
If you don't know, just guess.

## D
請只根據我貼上的課文回答，課文沒寫的就說「不知道」。
## D (EN)
Answer only from the text I pasted. If it's not in the text, say 'I don't know.'

## 解析
限定資料來源，AI 就不會亂加課文沒有的東西。
## Why
Limiting the source stops AI from adding things the text doesn't say.

## 提示
老師規定只能用什麼？
## Hint
Where must answers come from?

## B (EN)

Make 3 sentences using 'because'. Example: I took an umbrella because it rained.

## Why
An example sentence helps AI make exactly what you need.

## 提示
有沒有例句可以給 AI 參考？
## Hint
Is there an example you can give AI?
