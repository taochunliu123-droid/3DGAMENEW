# 取隊名 | Team Name
答案：A

## 情境
你們小隊要取一個隊名。
## Scenario
Your team needs a name.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我們想 5 個隊名，要像「閃電小隊」一樣短又有力。
## A (EN)
Please give us 5 team names, short and strong like 'Lightning Squad'.

## B
請幫我們想 5 個隊名，要很酷、很特別、很好聽。
## B (EN)
Please give us 5 team names that are cool, special and sound great.

## C
請幫我們想 5 個隊名，越長越好，要有很多意思。
## C (EN)
Please give us 5 team names, as long as possible with lots of meaning.

## D
請幫我們想 5 個隊名，要跟其他隊都不一樣。
## D (EN)
Please give us 5 team names that are different from every other team.

## 解析
給一個例子，AI 就知道你要的感覺。「很酷很特別」每個人想的不一樣；越長越難喊；AI 不知道別隊叫什麼。
## Why
An example shows the feeling you want. 'Cool and special' means different things to everyone, long names are hard to shout, and AI doesn't know other teams' names.

## 提示
可以給 AI 一個喜歡的例子嗎？
## Hint
Can you give AI an example you like?

# 只看課文 | Only the Text
答案：D

## 情境
老師說只能用課文回答問題。
## Scenario
Your teacher says answers must come only from the lesson text.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請根據課文回答，如果課文寫得不夠清楚，就上網多找一些資料補充。
## A (EN)
Answer from the text, and if it's not clear enough, look online for more info to add.

## B
請根據課文回答，答得越詳細越好，可以自己延伸。
## B (EN)
Answer from the text in as much detail as possible. Feel free to add more.

## C
請盡量回答，不確定的地方用你知道的知識補上。
## C (EN)
Answer as best you can, and fill gaps with what you know.

## D
請只根據我貼上的課文回答，課文沒寫的就說「不知道」。
## D (EN)
Answer only from the text I pasted. If it's not in the text, say 'I don't know.'

## 解析
老師規定只能用課文，就要限定來源，並說好沒寫的怎麼辦。上網補充、自己延伸、用 AI 的知識補，都會超出課文。
## Why
Only the text is allowed, so limit the source and say what to do if it's missing. Searching online, adding more or filling gaps all go beyond the text.

## 提示
老師規定只能用什麼？
## Hint
Where must answers come from?

# 因為所以 | Using Because
答案：C

## 情境
你要用「因為……所以……」造句。
## Scenario
You need to make sentences with 'because'.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請用「因為……所以……」造 3 個又長又難的句子。
## A (EN)
Make 3 long, difficult sentences using 'because'.

## B
請用「因為……所以……」造 3 個句子，越特別、越有創意、越讓人驚訝越好。
## B (EN)
Make 3 sentences using 'because'. The more unusual, creative and surprising the better.

## C
請用「因為……所以……」造 3 個句子，例如：因為下雨，所以我帶傘。
## C (EN)
Make 3 sentences using 'because'. Example: I took an umbrella because it rained.

## D
請解釋「因為……所以……」的意思和用法。
## D (EN)
Please explain what 'because' means and how to use it.

## 解析
給例句，AI 造出來的句子就會跟你要的一樣簡單清楚。又長又難、越特別越好沒有標準；解釋用法不是造句。
## Why
An example sentence gets exactly the kind you need. 'Long and hard' or 'unusual' has no standard; explaining usage isn't making sentences.

## 提示
有沒有例句可以給 AI 參考？
## Hint
Is there an example you can give AI?

# 不要可怕 | Nothing Scary
答案：B

## 情境
弟弟怕可怕的故事。
## Scenario
Your little brother is afraid of scary stories.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫一個冒險故事，要有很多刺激、驚險的情節。
## A (EN)
Please write an adventure story full of thrilling, dangerous moments.

## B
請寫一個冒險故事，不要有可怕或打鬥的情節。
## B (EN)
Please write an adventure story with nothing scary and no fighting.

## C
請寫一個冒險故事，主角要很勇敢、很厲害。
## C (EN)
Please write an adventure story with a brave, powerful hero.

## D
請寫一個冒險故事，最後壞人要被打敗。
## D (EN)
Please write an adventure story where the villain gets defeated.

## 解析
說出「不要什麼」，AI 才會避開弟弟怕的內容。刺激驚險、打敗壞人都可能出現可怕或打鬥；主角勇敢沒有排除任何東西。
## Why
Saying what to avoid keeps out what your brother fears. Danger or defeating a villain may bring fights, and a brave hero rules nothing out.

## 提示
有什麼是一定不能出現的？
## Hint
What must not appear?

# 選哪本書 | Which Book?
答案：A

## 情境
你在兩本書之間猶豫。
## Scenario
You can't decide between two books.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請用「好不好讀、有沒有圖、多長」比較這兩本書。
## A (EN)
Compare these two books by: easy to read, pictures, and length.

## B
請比較這兩本書，告訴我哪一本比較好。
## B (EN)
Please compare these two books and tell me which is better.

## C
請比較這兩本書，把兩本的內容都完整講一遍。
## C (EN)
Please compare these two books by retelling both stories in full.

## D
請比較這兩本書，看哪一本得過比較多獎。
## D (EN)
Please compare these two books by which won more awards.

## 解析
用你在乎的標準比較，結果才對你有用。「哪本比較好」沒有標準；完整講內容太長；得獎多不一定適合你。
## Why
Compare by what you care about. 'Which is better' has no criteria, retelling is too long, and awards don't mean it suits you.

## 提示
你用什麼標準選書？
## Hint
How do you choose books?

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我寫一篇日記，格式你自己決定就好。

# 黑熊介紹 | Black Bear Card
答案：C

## 情境
介紹要貼在小卡上，只能寫 50 字。
## Scenario
The intro goes on a small card with room for only 50 words.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請介紹台灣黑熊，重點要清楚、寫得好一點。
## A (EN)
Introduce the Formosan black bear clearly and nicely.

## B
請盡量詳細地介紹台灣黑熊，我再自己刪短。
## B (EN)
Introduce the Formosan black bear in full detail, and I'll cut it down.

## C
請用 50 字以內介紹台灣黑熊。
## C (EN)
Introduce the Formosan black bear in 50 words or fewer.

## D
請介紹台灣黑熊，要寫到牠的所有特徵。
## D (EN)
Introduce the Formosan black bear, covering all its features.

## 解析
小卡只放得下 50 字，直接限制字數最準。「寫好一點」沒有長度；寫長再刪很費工；所有特徵一定放不下。
## Why
The card fits 50 words, so set the limit. 'Nicely' has no length, cutting it yourself is extra work, and every feature won't fit.

## 提示
小卡能寫多少字？
## Hint
How much fits on the card?

# 水果謎語 | Fruit Riddles
答案：B

## 情境
你想出謎語給同學猜。
## Scenario
You want riddles for your classmates.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請出 3 個水果謎語，要很難，讓同學都猜不到。
## A (EN)
Give me 3 fruit riddles so hard nobody can guess them.

## B
請出 3 個水果謎語，例如：紅紅圓圓，咬一口甜甜（蘋果）。
## B (EN)
Give me 3 fruit riddles. Example: Red and round, sweet when you bite (apple).

## C
請出 3 個水果謎語，要很有趣、很好笑。
## C (EN)
Give me 3 fruit riddles that are really funny.

## D
請出 3 個謎語給我，什麼主題都可以。
## D (EN)
Give me 3 riddles about anything.

## 解析
示範一個，AI 就知道謎語要多難、長什麼樣子。「很難」「很好笑」沒有標準；什麼主題都可以就不是水果謎語。
## Why
One example shows how hard and what shape. 'Hard' or 'funny' has no standard, and 'anything' isn't fruit riddles.

## 提示
可以先示範一個給 AI 看嗎？
## Hint
Can you show AI one first?
