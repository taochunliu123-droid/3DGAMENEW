# 只看課文 | Only the Text
答案：B

## 情境
你請 AI 回答國語課〈小黑熊〉的問題。
## Scenario
You ask AI to answer questions about your reading lesson "Little Black Bear".

## 問題
哪一句才是 R「參考與限制」？
## Question
Which line is R (Refer: examples, limits, benchmarks)?

## A
這是國語課本第六課的課文。
## A (EN)
It's Lesson 6 in my reading book.

## B
只能根據課文裡寫的內容來回答。
## B (EN)
Answer only from what the text says.

## C
每一題都要用完整的句子回答。
## C (EN)
Answer each question in full sentences.

## D
你是一位很會帶閱讀的國語老師。
## D (EN)
You're a teacher who's great at reading lessons.

## 解析
「只能根據課文」是幫 AI 劃界線，不能用課文以外的東西，這就是限制（R）。
## Why
"Only from the text" draws a line AI can't cross — that's a limit (R).

## 提示
哪一句在幫 AI 劃界線？
## Hint
Which line draws a line for AI?

# 水果謎語 | Fruit Riddles
答案：D

## 情境
你要請 AI 出水果謎語。四句裡有三句是例子或限制。
## Scenario
You want AI to make fruit riddles. Three lines are examples or limits.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 R「參考與限制」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT R (Refer: examples, limits, benchmarks). Which one?

## A
像這樣出：「紅紅圓圓，咬一口甜甜」。
## A (EN)
Like this: "Red and round, sweet when you bite."

## B
答案只能是我們吃過的水果。
## B (EN)
Answers must be fruits we've eaten.

## C
不要出跟上次重複的水果。
## C (EN)
Don't repeat fruits from last time.

## D
語氣要好玩，一題寫成一行就好。
## D (EN)
Make it fun, one riddle per line.

## 解析
「好玩、一題一行」是語氣和格式（T）。給一題示範、只能用吃過的、不能重複，都是 R。
## Why
"Fun, one per line" is tone and format (T). An example, "only fruits we ate" and "no repeats" are R.

## 提示
哪一句沒有「示範」也沒有「不能」？
## Hint
Which line has no example and no "don't"?

# 黑熊睡多久 | Made-Up Fact
答案：A

## 情境
AI 說「黑熊一天要睡二十小時」，你查書發現根本沒有這種說法。
## Scenario
AI said black bears sleep 20 hours a day, but your book says nothing like that.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
不知道的就說不知道，不要自己編。
## A (EN)
If you don't know, say so. Don't make it up.

## B
你是在動物園照顧黑熊的保育員。
## B (EN)
You're a zookeeper who cares for black bears.

## C
這是要寫在學校動物小卡上的資料。
## C (EN)
This is for a school animal card.

## D
回答完，再用一句話幫我整理重點。
## D (EN)
Then sum up in one sentence.

## 解析
AI 有時候會「編」答案。直接規定不知道就說不知道，是最清楚的限制。當保育員不保證它不亂編。
## Why
AI sometimes invents answers. "Say you don't know" is the clearest limit. A zookeeper role doesn't stop that.

## 提示
哪一句是在定「規則」？
## Hint
Which line sets a rule?

# 作文打分數 | Grade My Essay
答案：D

## 情境
你請 AI 看看作文寫得好不好，它只說「很棒！」
## Scenario
You asked AI if your essay is good, and it only said "Great!"

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
你是一位改作文很嚴格的老師。
## A (EN)
You're a very strict essay teacher.

## B
這篇作文的題目是〈我的家人〉。
## B (EN)
The topic is "My Family".

## C
目標是讓我下次作文多拿兩分。
## C (EN)
I want two more points next time.

## D
請照老師的評分表逐項打分。
## D (EN)
Score each item using the teacher's rubric.

## 解析
沒有比較標準，AI 只能說「很棒」。給它評分表當基準，它才能說出哪裡好、哪裡不夠。嚴格的老師沒有標準，也只是比較兇。
## Why
Without a standard, AI just says "great". A rubric gives it a benchmark. A strict teacher with no standard is just harsher.

## 提示
AI 要「拿什麼來比」？
## Hint
What should AI compare against?

# 社團報名 | Club Sign-Up
答案：A

## 情境
你要請 AI 幫你寫美術社報名表的自我介紹。四句裡有三句是參考或限制。
## Scenario
You want AI to write your art-club sign-up intro. Three lines are references or limits.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 R「參考與限制」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT R (Refer: examples, limits, benchmarks). Which one?

## A
希望社團老師看完就想選我。
## A (EN)
The club teacher should want to pick me.

## B
不要寫出我家的地址和電話。
## B (EN)
Don't include my address or phone number.

## C
參考學長去年寫的那份自我介紹。
## C (EN)
Use the intro an older student wrote last year.

## D
只寫跟畫畫有關的興趣就好。
## D (EN)
Only include hobbies about drawing.

## 解析
「希望老師想選我」是目標（O）。不能寫地址、參考學長的、只寫畫畫，都是 R。
## Why
"The teacher wants to pick me" is the goal (O). No address, the older student's intro and "only drawing" are R.

## 提示
哪一句是「希望發生的事」？
## Hint
Which line is something you hope happens?

# 心得像網路簡介 | Copied Summary
答案：B

## 情境
你請 AI 幫你寫讀書心得，結果寫得跟網路上的書本簡介幾乎一樣。
## Scenario
AI wrote your book report, but it reads like an online summary.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
我讀的是《小王子》，讀了兩次。
## A (EN)
I read The Little Prince twice.

## B
只能擴寫我自己寫的三句感想。
## B (EN)
Only expand the three thoughts I wrote.

## C
你是很會寫心得的高年級學姐。
## C (EN)
You're an older student great at book reports.

## D
先寫故事大綱，再寫心得。
## D (EN)
Write a summary first, then thoughts.

## 解析
心得要是「你的」想法。限制 AI 只能用你寫的感想來擴寫，它就不會去抄網路的內容。
## Why
A report should hold your thoughts. Limiting AI to your own notes stops it from copying online text.

## 提示
要怎麼讓 AI 只用「你的」想法？
## Hint
How can you make AI use only your thoughts?
