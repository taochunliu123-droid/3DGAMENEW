# 海龜報告 | Sea Turtle Talk
答案：D

## 情境
你要向全班報告海龜。
## Scenario
You'll give a report on sea turtles to your class.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我寫一份海龜報告，內容要多、圖片要漂亮。
## A (EN)
Please write a sea turtle report with lots of content and pretty pictures.

## B
請幫我寫海龜報告，目標是讓老師覺得我很用功。
## B (EN)
Please write a sea turtle report. The goal is to make the teacher think I work hard.

## C
請幫我寫一份海龜報告，把海龜的種類全部介紹一遍。
## C (EN)
Please write a sea turtle report introducing every kind of sea turtle.

## D
請幫我寫海龜報告，目標是讓同學知道怎麼保護海龜。
## D (EN)
Please help me write a sea turtle report. The goal is for classmates to learn how to protect sea turtles.

## 解析
目標是讓同學學會保護海龜，報告就有重點。內容多、介紹全部種類沒有方向；讓老師覺得你用功不是報告要達成的事。
## Why
The goal (classmates learn to protect turtles) gives focus. Lots of content or every species has no direction; impressing the teacher isn't the report's purpose.

## 提示
報告完，你希望同學學到什麼？
## Hint
What should classmates learn by the end?

# 班長演講 | Class Leader Speech

## 情境
你要競選班長。
## Scenario
You're running for class leader.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我寫班長演講，要用很多成語，聽起來很厲害。
## A (EN)
Please write my class leader speech with lots of fancy words so I sound smart.

## B

## 提示

## Hint

# 修改作文 | Fix My Essay
答案：B

## 情境
你想讓自己的作文變好，但還是你寫的。
## Scenario
You want a better essay that's still yours.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我把作文改成可以參加比賽得獎的程度，怎麼改都可以。
## A (EN)
Please make my essay good enough to win a writing contest. Change anything you want.

## B
請幫我改作文，讓句子更通順，但不要改掉我的想法。
## B (EN)
Please improve my essay so the sentences flow better, but keep my ideas.

## C
請幫我改作文，把所有句子都換成更高級的詞。
## C (EN)
Please improve my essay by replacing every sentence with fancier words.

## D
請幫我改作文，改完告訴我總共改了幾個地方。
## D (EN)
Please fix my essay and tell me how many places you changed.

## 解析
說清楚要改到什麼程度：句子通順、想法保留。「怎麼改都可以」「全換高級詞」會變成 AI 的作文；只要數字沒說要改好什麼。
## Why
Say how much to change: smoother sentences, same ideas. 'Change anything' or fancy words make it AI's essay; a count doesn't say what to improve.

## 提示
你要 AI 改一點，還是全部重寫？
## Hint
A small fix or a full rewrite?

# 乘法練習 | Times Tables
答案：A

## 情境
你想檢查自己懂不懂乘法。
## Scenario
You want to check if you understand multiplication.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請出 5 題乘法讓我練習，我寫完再給答案。
## A (EN)
Give me 5 multiplication problems to practice, and show the answers after I finish.

## B
請出 5 題乘法，題目和答案一起給我，方便我對。
## B (EN)
Give me 5 multiplication problems with the answers right next to them.

## C
請出 5 題最難的乘法題給我，越難越好。
## C (EN)
Give me the 5 hardest multiplication problems possible.

## D
請把九九乘法表整理成表格，讓我多看幾遍。
## D (EN)
Please make the times tables into a chart so I can read it again.

## 解析
目標是檢查自己懂不懂，所以先寫再看答案。答案一起給就沒辦法檢查；最難的題不一定測得出；看表格是背，不是檢查。
## Why
The goal is checking yourself, so answer first, then see answers. Answers next to questions ruin the check; hardest isn't a fair test; a chart is memorizing, not checking.

## 提示
先看答案，還能檢查自己嗎？
## Hint
Can you check yourself if you see the answers first?

# 去動物園 | To the Zoo
答案：D

## 情境
你想說服爸媽週末帶你去動物園。
## Scenario
You want to convince your parents to go to the zoo this weekend.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我寫一篇介紹動物園有多好玩的文章，寫得越長越好。
## A (EN)
Please write an article about how much fun the zoo is. Make it as long as possible.

## B
請幫我查這個週末動物園的開放時間和票價。
## B (EN)
Please look up the zoo's hours and ticket prices this weekend.

## C
請幫我想 3 個理由，說明我為什麼最喜歡動物。
## C (EN)
Please think of 3 reasons why animals are my favorite.

## D
請幫我想 3 個理由，讓爸媽答應週末去動物園。
## D (EN)
Help me think of 3 reasons so my parents agree to go to the zoo this weekend.

## 解析
目標是讓爸媽答應，理由要能說服他們。長文章、查票價、說你喜歡動物，都沒有直接回答爸媽「為什麼要去」。
## Why
The goal is getting a yes, so reasons must persuade parents. A long article, ticket info or why you love animals doesn't answer 'why go'.

## 提示
你想要的結果是什麼？
## Hint
What result do you want?

# 分組報告 | Group Project
答案：C

## 情境
你們 4 個人要一起做報告。
## Scenario
Four of you are doing a report together.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
我們 4 人要做報告，請幫忙分工，讓最厲害的人做最多。
## A (EN)
Four of us are doing a report. Split the work so the best student does most.

## B
我們 4 人要做報告，請直接幫我們把報告寫好。
## B (EN)
Four of us are doing a report. Please just write it for us.

## C
我們 4 人要做報告，請幫忙分工，讓每個人都有事做。
## C (EN)
Four of us are doing a report. Please split the work so everyone has a job.

## D
我們 4 人要做報告，請列出報告需要的所有資料。
## D (EN)
Four of us are doing a report. Please list all the materials we need.

## 解析
目標是每個人都有事做，AI 才會想到公平分工。讓最厲害的做最多不公平；直接寫好大家沒學到；列資料沒有分工。
## Why
The goal 'everyone has a job' leads to fair splitting. Giving the best student most is unfair, writing it teaches nothing, and a list isn't a split.

## 提示
分工要達成什麼？
## Hint
What should the split achieve?

# 畫畫比賽 | Art Contest
答案：B

## 情境
你要參加「未來城市」畫畫比賽。
## Scenario
You're entering a 'Future City' drawing contest.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
我要畫「未來城市」參加比賽，請直接幫我畫一張得獎作品。
## A (EN)
I'm drawing a 'Future City' for a contest. Please draw a winning picture for me.

## B
我要畫「未來城市」參加比賽，請給我 3 個有創意、別人不容易想到的點子。
## B (EN)
I'm drawing a 'Future City' for a contest. Please give me 3 creative ideas others won't think of.

## C
我要畫「未來城市」參加比賽，請介紹未來城市的歷史。
## C (EN)
I'm drawing a 'Future City' for a contest. Tell me the history of future cities.

## D
我要畫「未來城市」參加比賽，請告訴我大家最常畫、最多人想得到的點子有哪些。
## D (EN)
I'm drawing a 'Future City' for a contest. Tell me which ideas are the most common ones people draw.

## 解析
比賽要有創意，所以要「別人不容易想到」的點子。直接畫好就不是你的作品；歷史用不上；最多人畫的點子不特別。
## Why
A contest needs originality, so ask for uncommon ideas. A finished drawing isn't yours, history won't help, and popular ideas aren't special.

## 提示
你要 AI 幫你畫，還是幫你想？
## Hint
Do you want AI to draw for you or think with you?

# 一分鐘故事 | One-Minute Story
答案：A

## 情境
你要上台講故事，只有 1 分鐘。
## Scenario
You'll tell a story on stage and only have 1 minute.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫一個能在 1 分鐘內講完的故事。
## A (EN)
Please write a story I can tell in under 1 minute.

## B
請寫一個很精彩、有很多角色和轉折的故事。
## B (EN)
Please write an exciting story with lots of characters and twists.

## C
請寫一個故事，要有開頭、中間和結尾。
## C (EN)
Please write a story with a beginning, middle and end.

## D
請寫一個故事，寫完後幫我算有幾個字。
## D (EN)
Please write a story and count the words when you're done.

## 解析
上台只有 1 分鐘，要先說好「怎樣才算完成」。很多角色和轉折會太長；有頭有尾每個故事都有；算字數沒有限制長度。
## Why
With 1 minute on stage, say what 'done' means. Many characters and twists run long, every story has a start and end, and counting words doesn't limit length.

## 提示
上台時間有多長？
## Hint
How much time do you have on stage?
