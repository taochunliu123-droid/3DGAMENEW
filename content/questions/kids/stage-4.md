# 海龜報告 | Sea Turtle Talk
答案：D

## 情境
你要向全班報告海龜。
## Scenario
You'll give a report on sea turtles to your class.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫海龜。
## A (EN)
Sea turtles.

## B
請寫很多很多海龜知識。
## B (EN)
Write lots and lots of sea turtle facts.

## C
請寫一個海龜故事。
## C (EN)
Write a sea turtle story.

## D
請幫我寫海龜報告，目標是讓同學知道怎麼保護海龜。
## D (EN)
Please help me write a sea turtle report. The goal is for classmates to learn how to protect sea turtles.

## 解析
說出目標「讓同學知道怎麼保護海龜」，報告就會有重點。
## Why
Stating the goal gives the report a clear focus.

## 提示
報告完，你希望同學學到什麼？
## Hint
What should classmates learn by the end?

# 修改作文 | Fix My Essay
答案：B

## 情境
你想讓自己的作文變好，但還是你寫的。
## Scenario
You want a better essay that's still yours.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請幫我重寫整篇作文。
## A (EN)
Please rewrite my whole essay.

## B
請幫我改作文，讓句子更通順，但不要改掉我的想法。
## B (EN)
Please improve my essay so the sentences flow better, but keep my ideas.

## C
請幫我改。
## C (EN)
Please fix it.

## D
請寫一篇新的作文給我。
## D (EN)
Please write a new essay for me.

## 解析
說清楚要改到什麼程度，AI 才不會把你的作文換成它的。
## Why
Saying how much to change stops AI from replacing your essay with its own.

## 提示
你要 AI 改一點，還是全部重寫？
## Hint
A small fix or a full rewrite?

# 乘法練習 | Times Tables
答案：A

## 情境
你想檢查自己懂不懂乘法。
## Scenario
You want to check if you understand multiplication.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請出 5 題乘法讓我練習，我寫完再給答案。
## A (EN)
Give me 5 multiplication problems to practice, and show the answers after I finish.

## B

## B (EN)
Tell me the multiplication answers.

## C

## C (EN)

## D (EN)

## 提示

## Hint
Can you check yourself if you see the answers first?

# 一分鐘故事 | One-Minute Story
答案：A

## 情境
你要上台講故事，只有 1 分鐘。
## Scenario
You'll tell a story on stage and only have 1 minute.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫一個能在 1 分鐘內講完的故事。
## A (EN)
Please write a story I can tell in under 1 minute.

## B
請寫一個故事。
## B (EN)
Please write a story.

## C
請寫一個最長的故事。
## C (EN)
Please write the longest story.

## D
請寫一個故事大綱。
## D (EN)
Please write a story outline.

## 解析
說出「怎樣才算好」：1 分鐘內講完，長度就剛剛好。
## Why
Saying what 'good' means — under 1 minute — gets the right length.

## 提示
上台時間有多長？
## Hint
How much time do you have on stage?
