# 掃地輪值 | Cleaning Duty
答案：B

## 情境
你要請 AI 幫班上排掃地輪值表。
## Scenario
You want AI to make a cleaning schedule for your class.

## 問題
哪一句才是 O「目標」？
## Question
Which line is O (Goal: purpose & what "done" means)?

## A
我們班有二十八個人，分成七組。
## A (EN)
We have 28 students in seven groups.

## B
每個人每週掃地的次數要一樣多。
## B (EN)
Everyone cleans the same number of times each week.

## C
做成一週一張的表格，貼在後面。
## C (EN)
One table per week, posted at the back.

## D
你是一位做事很公平的衛生股長。
## D (EN)
You're a fair class cleaning leader.

## 解析
「每個人次數一樣多」是怎樣才算排得好，這就是目標（O）。「很公平的股長」只是角色，沒說怎樣算公平。
## Why
"Same number for everyone" defines a good schedule (O). "A fair leader" is just a role and doesn't define fair.

## 提示
哪一句可以拿來檢查表排得好不好？
## Hint
Which line could you use to check the schedule?

# 科展 | Science Fair

## Scenario

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 O「目標」，是哪一句？

One of these is NOT O (Goal: purpose & what "done" means). Which one?

# 下課遊戲 | Recess Game
答案：A

## 情境
你請 AI 幫你想班上下課的遊戲，它想了二十個，你不知道要選哪個。
## Scenario
You asked AI for a recess game; it gave twenty and you can't choose.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
我只需要一個今天下課就能玩的遊戲。
## A (EN)
I need just one game we can play at recess today.

## B
我們班有三十個人，教室不大。
## B (EN)
There are 30 of us and the room is small.

## C
你是很會帶活動的營隊大哥哥。
## C (EN)
You're a camp counselor great at games.

## D
每一個遊戲都只用一句話簡單介紹就好。
## D (EN)
Describe each game in one sentence.

## 解析
AI 不知道你要的「結果」是什麼，所以拼命多給。說清楚你只要一個、今天就能玩，它就會幫你挑。
## Why
AI didn't know what result you wanted, so it gave lots. Say you need one for today, and it will choose.

## 提示
你最後想要幾個？要拿來做什麼？
## Hint
How many do you want, and for what?

# 報告夠好了嗎 | Good Enough?
答案：D

## 情境
你請 AI 幫你寫恐龍報告，它寫得很長，你卻不知道這樣算不算好。
## Scenario
AI wrote a long dinosaur report, but you can't tell if it's good.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
你是在恐龍博物館工作的導覽員。
## A (EN)
You're a guide at a dinosaur museum.

## B
報告要用條列，配上一張圖。
## B (EN)
Use bullets and one picture.

## C

## Scenario
You want AI to help tidy your room. Three lines say what you want to achieve.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 O「目標」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT O (Goal: purpose & what "done" means). Which one?

## A

## B
整理完要讓我自己找得到東西。

# 跳繩練習 | Jump Rope
答案：C

## 情境
你請 AI 幫你安排跳繩練習。
## Scenario
You ask AI to plan your jump-rope practice.

## 問題
哪一句才是 O「目標」？
## Question
Which line is O (Goal: purpose & what "done" means)?

## A
我現在最多只能連跳二十下。
## A (EN)
Right now I can do only 20.

## B
每天練什麼，用一張表格寫出來。
## B (EN)
Put each day's practice in a table.

## C
希望一個月後可以連續跳一百下。

## D
先教我暖身，再教跳的方法。

"100 in a month" is the goal, with a number to check. "20 now" is the current state (C). Both have numbers.

## 提示
兩句都有數字，哪一句是「以後」？
## Hint
Two lines have numbers — which is about later?

# 明天小考 | Tomorrow's Quiz
答案：B

## 情境
你請 AI 幫你準備明天的小考，它把整本書都整理了一遍。
## Scenario
You asked AI to help with tomorrow's quiz, and it summarized the whole book.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
小考有二十題，都是選擇題。
## A (EN)
The quiz has 20 multiple-choice questions.

## B
目標是明天第三課考八十分。
## B (EN)
The goal is 80 on tomorrow's Lesson 3 quiz.

## C
請用問答的方式幫我整理。
## C (EN)
Organize it as questions and answers.

## D
你是很會猜考題的補習班老師。
## D (EN)
You're a tutor good at predicting test questions.

## 解析
AI 不知道你的目標，只好全部整理。說出「第三課、八十分」，它才知道要準備到哪裡、多深。
## Why
Without a goal, AI covered everything. "Lesson 3, score 80" tells it what and how much.

## 提示
AI 知道你要考什麼、考多好嗎？
## Hint
Does AI know what the quiz is and what score you want?
