# 給阿嬤的信 | Letter to Grandma
答案：C

## 情境
阿嬤看不懂太難的字。
## Scenario
Grandma can't read hard words.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請用正式公文寫信給阿嬤。
## A (EN)
Please write to Grandma like a formal notice.

## B
請用英文寫信給阿嬤。
## B (EN)
Please write to Grandma in French.

## C
請用簡單、溫暖的話寫一封給阿嬤的信。
## C (EN)
Please write a letter to Grandma in simple, warm words.

## D
請用很多成語寫信給阿嬤。
## D (EN)
Please write to Grandma with lots of fancy words.

## 解析
說出「簡單、溫暖」，信的語氣才適合阿嬤。
## Why
Saying 'simple and warm' makes the letter right for Grandma.

## 提示
阿嬤喜歡什麼樣的說話方式？
## Hint
How does Grandma like people to talk?

# 複習重點 | Review Notes

## Scenario
You want to sum up this lesson for review.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A

## A (EN)

## B (EN)
