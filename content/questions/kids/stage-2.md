# 自我介紹 | Introduce Yourself
答案：B

## 情境
你要在新班級自我介紹。
## Scenario
You need to introduce yourself to a new class.

## 問題
哪一句能讓介紹最像你？
## Question
Which one makes the intro sound most like you?

## A
請幫我寫自我介紹。
## A (EN)
Please write a self-introduction.

## B
我四年級，喜歡畫畫和籃球，請幫我寫 30 秒的自我介紹。
## B (EN)
I'm in 4th grade and I like drawing and basketball. Please write a 30-second self-introduction.

## C
請寫一篇很棒的自我介紹。
## C (EN)
Please write an awesome self-introduction.

## D
請寫一篇長長的自我介紹。
## D (EN)
Please write a long self-introduction.

## 解析
告訴 AI 你的年級和喜好，寫出來才會像你，不是別人。
## Why
Telling AI your grade and hobbies makes the intro sound like you, not someone else.

## 提示
AI 認識你嗎？要先告訴它什麼？
## Hint
Does AI know you? What should you tell it first?
