# 活動企劃 | Event Proposal
答案：B

## 情境
你要請 AI 寫一份員工活動企劃。
## Scenario
You want AI to write a staff event proposal.

## 問題
哪一句才是 S「步驟」？
## Question
Which line is S (Steps: process & checkpoints)?

## A
企劃書要分成背景、做法、預算三個段落。
## A (EN)
The proposal has three parts: background, plan, budget.

## B
先列三個方向給我選，選好再寫細節。
## B (EN)
Give me three directions first; write details after I pick.

## C
選定的方向要能在兩個月內辦完。
## C (EN)
The chosen idea must be doable within two months.

## D
你是辦過很多大型活動的企劃主任。
## D (EN)
You're a planning lead who has run many big events.

## 解析
「先列方向、選好再寫細節」安排了先後順序和一個確認點，這是 S。分三段是成品格式（T），兩個月內是限制（R）。
## Why
"Directions first, details after I pick" sets order and a checkpoint (S). Three parts is format (T); two months is a limit (R).

## 提示
哪一句讓你可以中途選擇？
## Hint
Which line lets you choose midway?

# 步驟陷阱 | Step Trap
答案：D

## 情境
你要請 AI 寫年度檢討報告。四句裡有三句是在安排步驟或檢查點。
## Scenario
You want AI to write an annual review. Three lines set steps or checkpoints.

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 S「步驟」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT S (Steps: process & checkpoints). Which one?

## A
先讀完全部資料，再開始動筆。
## A (EN)
Read all the material before writing.

## B
每寫完一段，就停下來問我要不要改。
## B (EN)
After each section, stop and ask if I want changes.

## C
最後對照原始數字，檢查一次總和。
## C (EN)
At the end, check the totals against the raw numbers.

## D
答案用 1、2、3 編號條列出來。
## D (EN)
Number the answer 1, 2, 3 as a list.

## 解析
「用 1、2、3 編號」看起來像步驟，其實只是成品的排版，屬於 T。另外三句都在安排 AI 做事的順序和檢查點。
## Why
"Number it 1, 2, 3" looks like steps but is just layout (T). The other three set AI's order of work and checks.

## 提示
有數字編號，不一定就是步驟。
## Hint
Numbering doesn't always mean steps.

# 發票加錯 | Wrong Total
答案：A

## 情境
AI 幫你加總十張發票的金額，結果加錯了。
## Scenario
AI added up ten receipts and got the total wrong.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
先逐張列出金額，再加總，最後驗算一次。
## A (EN)
List each amount, add them up, then double-check.

## B
你是做事非常細心、從不出錯的資深會計。
## B (EN)
You're a meticulous senior accountant who never errs.

## C
這十張發票都是這個月的出差費用。
## C (EN)
All ten are this month's travel expenses.

## D
請用表格呈現，最下面一列寫總計。
## D (EN)
Use a table with the total in the last row.

## 解析
要減少算錯，靠的是過程：一張張列出來、再加、再驗算。說它「從不出錯」不會讓它真的不出錯。
## Why
Fewer mistakes come from process: list, add, verify. Saying "never errs" doesn't make it so.

## 提示
怎樣才能「抓到」錯？
## Hint
How would the error get caught?

## Scenario

## 問題
四句裡有一句不是 S「步驟」，是哪一句？
## Question
One of these is NOT S (Steps: process & checkpoints). Which one?

## A (EN)

## B

# 問卷分析 | Survey Analysis
答案：C

## 情境
你要請 AI 分析 300 份活動問卷。
## Scenario
You want AI to analyze 300 event surveys.

## 問題
哪一句才是 S「步驟」？
## Question
Which line is S (Steps: process & checkpoints)?

## A
結論用三個重點條列，每點一句話。
## A (EN)
Conclude in three bullet points, one sentence each.

## B
只根據這 300 份問卷，不要推論其他。
## B (EN)
Use only these 300 surveys; don't infer beyond them.

## C
先分類開放題，再算比例，最後才寫結論。
## C (EN)
Sort the open answers, then compute shares, then conclude.

## D
分析是為了決定明年要不要續辦。
## D (EN)
It's to decide whether to hold the event next year.

## 解析
「先分類、再算比例、最後寫結論」是 AI 做事的順序（S）。三點條列是 T，只根據 300 份是 R，續不續辦是 O。
## Why
"Sort, compute, then conclude" is AI's sequence (S). Three bullets is T, only 300 surveys is R, next year's decision is O.

## 提示
哪一句在安排 AI「先做什麼、後做什麼」？
## Hint
Which line orders what AI does first and next?

# 一次塞太多 | Too Much at Once
答案：B

## 情境
你一次叫 AI「翻譯、摘要、再寫回信」，結果三件事都做得馬馬虎虎。
## Scenario
You asked AI to translate, summarize and reply all at once, and all three came out sloppy.

## 問題
最該補上哪一句？
## Question
Which line should you add?

## A
你是精通中英文、反應很快的業務助理。
## A (EN)
You're a quick, bilingual sales assistant.

## B
先翻譯給我看，確認後再摘要，最後回信。
## B (EN)
Translate first; summarize after I check; reply last.

## C
這封信是德國最大的那位客戶寄來的。
## C (EN)
This email is from our biggest client in Germany.

## D
回信要讓客戶願意接受交期延後一週。
## D (EN)
The reply should get the client to accept a one-week delay.

## 解析
多件事擠在一起，每件都只做一半。拆成步驟、中間讓你確認，每一步才做得紮實。
## Why
Cramming tasks together halves the quality of each. Split them and check in between.

## 提示
三件事要一起做，還是一件一件來？
## Hint
All three at once, or one at a time?
