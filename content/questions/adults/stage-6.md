# 營收下滑 | Revenue Drop
答案：C

## 情境
這個月營收下降 15%。
## Scenario
Revenue fell 15% this month.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請直接給我三個能讓營收回升的方法。
## A (EN)
Just give me three ways to get revenue back up.

## B
請把這個月的營收數字做成圖表。
## B (EN)
Turn this month's revenue numbers into a chart.

## C
請先列出可能原因，再依影響大小排序，最後建議處理方式。
## C (EN)
First list possible causes, then rank them by impact, then suggest actions.

## D
請分析營收下降的原因，想到什麼就寫什麼，市場、產品、人員、季節都可以談。
## D (EN)
Analyze why revenue dropped. Write whatever comes to mind: market, product, staff, season, anything.

## 解析
先原因、再排序、後建議，思考才有條理。直接給方法沒找原因；做圖表沒分析；想到什麼寫什麼沒有順序。
## Why
Causes, ranking, then suggestions. Jumping to fixes skips causes, a chart isn't analysis, and free writing has no order.

## 提示
要 AI 照什麼順序想？
## Hint
In what order should AI think?

# 寫長報告 | Long Report
答案：B

## 情境
你要寫一份 10 頁的報告。
## Scenario
You need to write a 10-page report.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請直接寫完 10 頁報告，我再一次看完修改。
## A (EN)
Write the full 10 pages, and I'll review it all at once.

## B
請先給我大綱，我確認後再寫各段內容。
## B (EN)
Give me an outline first; after I approve it, write each section.

## C
請先寫結論，其他部分之後再補。
## C (EN)
Write the conclusion first and fill in the rest later.

## D
請給我 3 個報告標題讓我先挑。
## D (EN)
Give me 3 report titles to pick from first.

## 解析
先確認大綱，就不會寫完才發現方向錯。一次寫完改起來最累；先寫結論沒有架構；標題不是這一步的重點。
## Why
Approving an outline first avoids a wrong direction. All at once is hardest to fix, conclusion-first lacks structure, and titles aren't the point.

## 提示
哪一步可以先檢查？
## Hint
Which step can you check first?

# 導入新系統 | New System Rollout
答案：A

## 情境
公司要導入新的請假系統。
## Scenario
The company is rolling out a new leave system.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請把導入分成準備、試用、正式上線三階段，每階段列重點。
## A (EN)
Split the rollout into prepare, pilot, go-live, with key points for each.

## B
請列出導入新系統需要注意的 20 件事。
## B (EN)
List 20 things to watch out for in the rollout.

## C
請寫一封信，通知全公司所有部門，下週一起全部改用新的請假系統。
## C (EN)
Write an email telling every department to switch completely to the new leave system from next Monday.

## D
請比較新舊請假系統的功能差異。
## D (EN)
Compare the features of the old and new leave systems.

## 解析
分階段規劃，每一步要做什麼才清楚。20 件事沒有順序；直接全面上線風險高；功能比較不是導入計畫。
## Why
Phases make each step clear. 20 items have no order, switching everyone at once is risky, and a feature comparison isn't a rollout plan.

## 提示
可以分成幾個階段？
## Hint
How many phases can it split into?

# 自我檢查 | Self-Check
答案：D

## 情境
你給了 AI 三個要求。
## Scenario
You gave AI three requirements.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請寫仔細一點，不要出錯。
## A (EN)
Please be careful and don't make mistakes.

## B
請寫完後自己評分，滿分 10 分，如果低於 8 分就再寫一次。
## B (EN)
When done, rate your own work out of 10, and if it's under 8, write it again.

## C
請寫兩個版本讓我比較看看。
## C (EN)
Write two versions for me to compare.

## D
寫完後，請對照我的 3 個要求逐項檢查一次。
## D (EN)
When done, check the result against my 3 requirements one by one.

## 解析
加上具體的檢查步驟，比說「仔細一點」有效。「不要出錯」沒有方法；自評分數不會對照要求；兩個版本不等於有檢查。
## Why
A concrete check step beats 'be careful'. 'No mistakes' has no method, a self-score doesn't check requirements, and two versions aren't a check.

## 提示
怎麼讓 AI 自己檢查？
## Hint
How can AI check its own work?

# 選方案 | Choosing an Option
答案：C

## 情境
你在兩個方案間猶豫。
## Scenario
You're torn between two options.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請直接告訴我該選哪一個就好。
## A (EN)
Just tell me which one to pick.

## B
請把兩個方案各自的所有細節，包括成本、時程、人力都完整列出來。
## B (EN)
List every detail of both options, including cost, timeline and staffing, in full.

## C
請先列出每個方案的優缺點，再給建議和理由。
## C (EN)
First list pros and cons of each, then give a recommendation with reasons.

## D
請再給我第三個方案參考看看。
## D (EN)
Give me a third option to consider.

## 解析
先比較、再建議，你才看得到判斷過程。只給答案看不到理由；只列細節沒有結論；多一個方案讓選擇更難。
## Why
Compare, then recommend, so you see the reasoning. A bare pick has no reasons, details have no conclusion, and a third option makes it harder.

## 提示
想看到 AI 怎麼想的嗎？
## Hint
Do you want to see how AI thinks?

# 活動籌備 | Event Planning
答案：B

## 情境
一個月後要辦活動。
## Scenario
You're hosting an event in a month.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請列出辦這場活動需要做的所有事情。
## A (EN)
List everything needed to run the event.

## B
請從活動日往回推，列出前 4 週每週要完成的事。
## B (EN)
Work backward from the event date and list what to finish each of the 4 weeks before.

## C
請幫我寫一份完整的活動企劃書。
## C (EN)
Write a complete event proposal for me.

## D
請告訴我辦活動最容易出錯的地方。
## D (EN)
Tell me what usually goes wrong at events.

## 解析
倒推時程，每週該做什麼一目了然。所有事情沒有時間順序；企劃書和注意事項都不是時程。
## Why
Planning backward shows what's due each week. An everything-list has no timing; a proposal or pitfalls aren't a schedule.

## 提示
怎麼排時間最不會來不及？
## Hint
How do you schedule so nothing's late?

# 銷售資料 | Sales Data
答案：A

## 情境
一份銷售資料有重複和缺漏。
## Scenario
Sales data has duplicates and gaps.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請先刪掉重複資料，再統計各區銷售額，最後指出重點。
## A (EN)
First remove duplicates, then total sales by region, then point out key findings.

## B
請直接統計各區銷售額並做成圖表。
## B (EN)
Total sales by region and chart it right away.

## C
請找出銷售最好的那一區就好。
## C (EN)
Just find the best-selling region.

## D
請先指出重點，再回頭檢查資料。
## D (EN)
Point out key findings first, then check the data.

## 解析
先清理、再統計、後解讀，結果才正確。沒清掉重複資料就統計會算錯；只找最好的一區不夠；先下結論順序反了。
## Why
Clean, total, then interpret. Totaling before removing duplicates is wrong, one region isn't enough, and conclusions first is backward.

## 提示
資料要先處理什麼？
## Hint
What must be done to the data first?

# 客訴回信 | Complaint Reply
答案：D

## 情境
客戶抱怨出貨太慢。
## Scenario
A customer complains shipping is slow.

## 問題
哪一句最好？
## Question
Which one is best?

## A
請先詳細說明我們的出貨流程和這次延遲的經過，最後再跟客戶道歉。
## A (EN)
First explain our shipping process and what caused this delay in detail, then apologize at the end.

## B
請寫一封回信，把公司政策完整附上。
## B (EN)
Write a reply and attach the full company policy.

## C
請寫一封回信，直接提供折價券就好。
## C (EN)
Write a reply that just offers a coupon.

## D
請照「道歉→說明原因→提出補償」的順序寫回信。
## D (EN)
Write the reply in this order: apologize → explain the cause → offer compensation.

## 解析
先道歉、再說明、最後補償，客戶感受最好。先講流程像在找藉口；附政策太生硬；只給折價券沒有誠意也沒解釋。
## Why
Apologize, explain, compensate. Process first sounds like excuses, a policy dump is cold, and a coupon alone lacks sincerity.

## 提示
回信可以照什麼順序寫？
## Hint
What order could the reply follow?
